Two ways to enjoy your kimono

  • Select three preferred kimonos in advance.
  • On the day, see and compare your three selected kimonos in person at the Ginza shop before making your final choice.
  • Both dressing and undressing take place at the shop.
  • Please provide your height and shoe size beforehand.

  • There is no physical showroom. Choose one kimono in advance for delivery to your hotel.
  • A professional kimono dresser visits your hotel to help you dress.
  • After the experience, remove the kimono yourself at the hotel. Your tour guide arranges shipping back to the shop.
  • Please provide your height and shoe size beforehand.

Hair & Makeup Neither shop includes makeup or hair styling in the quoted prices. Both services can be arranged separately at an additional cost upon request.
